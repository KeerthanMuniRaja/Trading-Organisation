## Why it does not lose the trade

- **Fill price:** fills use the verified opening price of the first trading day after the review, and Monday's open is recorded in the end-of-day data.
- **Later run:** a cycle run after 16:00 IST on 20 October still fills them at the 19 October open.
- **Validity:** the proposals stay valid until 23 October.
- **Shrink-to-fit:** if a share had become dearer, a linked buy fills only as many reviewed shares as still fit the budget.

## Takeaway

A missed day costs nothing as long as a cycle runs before the proposals lapse. A schedule or an alert would make this automatic once the owner chooses one.
