# 21 October 2026

## Slower, tax-aware rules

Every R&D idea family now takes a rebalance interval (1, 3, 6 or 12 months): the decision is taken at the start of the period and held. Two pre-registered ideas, the live bot's own rule traded quarterly and yearly, joined the catalogue, which now has 20 ideas (bar 0.0025). A yearly rule is assumed to sell lots only after 12 months, so they pay the long-term rate.

On the 8 shares over 106 months, after an approximate tax:

| Rule | After tax |
| --- | --- |
| Momentum, monthly (the live bot) | 9.8% |
| Momentum, quarterly | 10.7% |
| Momentum, yearly | 12.3% |
| Holding the basket | 12.5% |

Slower trading recovers most of the loss but is still no edge ([roadmap](../skill-roadmap.md)). Moving the live bot to yearly rebalancing would be a new release and is the owner's decision.
