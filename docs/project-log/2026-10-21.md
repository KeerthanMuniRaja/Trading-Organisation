## Autonomy rehearsal and the starter policy

The owner deferred live operations to Saturday and asked for development toward a strong, autonomous system.

**Rehearsal** (`backend/test/rehearsal.test.ts`): the real trading path for 8 rounds, with 4 bots, standing approvals on, and failures injected:
- duplicate proposals and executions;
- a stale quote;
- another bot's credential;
- a losing bot for the policy to suspend;
- raises over the policy;
- a crash.

Every round ends with the money, limit, trading, brake, autonomy and audit invariants. All held. Buys accepted just before a suspension or the kill switch were refused at execution.

**Starter policy** (`docs/policies/autonomy-starter.json`): protective only. It suspends flagged bots, acknowledges watches, trials same-rule fixes and rolls back failing releases; no money moves by itself. It is ready for Saturday's setup, and a test checks that it is valid.

## The supervisor keeps the organisation running

Three gaps for unattended running were fixed:
- **Missed jobs:** a laptop asleep at 16:05 meant no cycle that day. The supervisor now catches up today's readiness (before the close) and cycle (after 16:05) when it starts or wakes, at most twice each, and counts a cycle run by hand.
- **Errors:** a job failing unexpectedly no longer ends the supervisor.
- **Liveness:** a heartbeat every minute (also during a long cycle) appears on the status screen, and readiness warns when it stops.

`scripts/install-supervisor-task.ps1` (owner-run, per user, with `-WhatIf` and `-Remove`) registers a Task Scheduler task that starts the supervisor at logon and restarts it whenever it stops.
