## The supervisor keeps the organisation running

Three gaps for unattended running were fixed:
- **Missed jobs:** a laptop asleep at 16:05 meant no cycle that day. The supervisor now catches up today's readiness (before the close) and cycle (after 16:05) when it starts or wakes, at most twice each, and counts a cycle run by hand.
- **Errors:** a job failing unexpectedly no longer ends the supervisor.
- **Liveness:** a heartbeat every minute (also during a long cycle) appears on the status screen, and readiness warns when it stops.

`scripts/install-supervisor-task.ps1` (owner-run, per user, with `-WhatIf` and `-Remove`) registers a Task Scheduler task that starts the supervisor at logon and restarts it whenever it stops.

## Closing the gaps found in the review

Gap-analysis items that needed no owner decision were built in three batches.

**Decision inputs are point-in-time and complete** (commit 761572b):
- the debate desk sees only headlines and prices from before the signal, compared as real instants across time zones;
- it also sees the bot's own scored record and whether the news and data were complete; the bull answers the bear;
- a scenario-panel voice that fails is now left out of the votes instead of counting as flat;
- the evaluation suite fails a task that did not run, flags regressions against a saved baseline and exits non-zero on DON'T SHIP;
- training data never mixes evaluation answers into training files;
- a stale or incomplete regime is marked in the briefing, the digest and the status line.

**A knowledge index connects what we know** (commit 495389a, [knowledge index](../knowledge-index.md)):
- one SQLite file with full-text search links shares, sources, news drivers, items, stories, the daily regime, ideas, method cards, problems and calls;
- `dossier(symbol, as_of)` answers "what did we know about this share on that day", point-in-time;
- the debate desk gets a compact press dossier from it; the cycle rebuilds it after the link study;
- first build: 2,692 nodes and 526 edges.

**Guards against luck** ([research methods](../research-methods.md)):
- every idea now gets a probabilistic and a deflated Sharpe ratio against all ideas tried, years ahead of the basket, the result at twice the costs, India's stress windows, and a random baseline matched to its turnover;
- 8 shares: the best deflated Sharpe is 0.22 (reversal), far below the usual 0.95;
- NIFTY 50 reversal: 0.31, ahead in 7 of 9 years and at twice the costs, but survivorship-biased. It is the one candidate worth re-testing on point-in-time membership;
- **regime v2**, closer to the trading-skills specification, also warns of falls but less sharply than v1, and calls SEVERE on 42% of days. v1 stays ([market regime](../market-regime.md)).

**Trust checks** ([operations cycle](../operations-cycle.md)):
- **run manifests:** every cycle run records its code commit and fingerprint, the switches it ran with, and the prices it left;
- **digest self-check:** "Can you trust this digest?" lists what to check first (no cycle, failed steps, stale or lagging prices, an old regime or old knowledge) and says when the code changed between runs;
- **review scorecard:** a read-only cycle step that compares the calls the risk review turned down with the calls it took, by rejection reason and by the regime when each call was made. It needs at least 20 scored calls on each side before it gives a verdict.

On today's laptop the self-check correctly says no cycle has run today (live operations are paused until Saturday).
