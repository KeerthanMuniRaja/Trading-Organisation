## The news was collected but never read

A check of the 27 Oct cycle found three steps skipped, every day, since the move:
- `claims-and-fact-check`, `headline-reader` and `chain-links` were all skipped. The owner configuration
  `.local/claims/autolearn.json` did not exist on this machine, and was not in the data repository either.
- So no news was ever fact-checked. The fact-checker is also what reviews the collected evidence, which is why 42
  items had waited over 24 hours.
- The headline reader, the chain links and the debate had no verified news.

**Fix:**
- The configuration is back. The verified news goes to `debate-bull`, `debate-bear` and `tradingagents`; no primary
  sources; held 7 days for corroboration; at most 20 model calls a cycle.
- STARTUP now checks for the file, so a move cannot lose it again, and `export-data` carries it.

**BusinessLine** failed once on 27 Oct (`FEED_UNREACHABLE`). A retry fetched 25 new items, so it was a one-off.

**Running the chain by hand found two more faults:**
- **The claims `.env` reader kept the quotes.** `PRINCIPALS_JSON` is quoted in `.env`; Node's `--env-file` strips the
  quotes, but `autolearn.env_file` did not, so a manual run stopped at once with a JSON error. The daily cycle was not
  affected, because it passes the cleaned value down. The reader now strips matching quotes as Node does.
- **Windows briefly locked a state file.** The fact-checker stopped mid-cycle on `Access is denied` while replacing
  `decisions.json`. Every atomic replace now retries for up to a few seconds, as the trading desk's `batch.save`
  already did: in claims, the knowledge index, TradingAgents and Hermes.

The first real run queued 149 news items. Two cycles then ran end to end, with the model reading 20 items in each:
- the model read 40 items: 3 claims accepted, 27 rejected by the quote check;
- the fact-checker decided 20: 18 had no checkable claim, and 2 were older than 7 days with no second source;
- no headline is verified yet, because that needs two independent sources reporting the same fact.

**Throughput.** 20 model calls took about 9 minutes, but about 75 items arrive a day, so the backlog could never
clear. The limit is now 80 calls a cycle (about 35 minutes), and the newest news is read first: today's news matters
most, and an item older than 7 days can no longer be corroborated in time.

**R&D:** a model timeout no longer fails the whole `rnd-scout` step. The model's new ideas wait for another day, the
failure is recorded (`model-failed`), and the catalogue is still screened. Both of 27 Oct's `rnd-scout` failures were
model timeouts.

**Rule bot release:** today's code is release v3, running as a canary under the standing approval to trial new
releases, so the bot may trade. The agenda item to make v3 fully active waits for the owner, or for the standing
approval to promote canaries once it has live results.

## Teaching: when momentum works

The lab now splits each factor's record by market condition. In the 4 months after the whole universe fell 15% or
more over 6 months:
- momentum-12-1 trailed the universe by 1.6% a month (ahead 1 of 4);
- last month's losers (reversal-1m) led by 3.1% a month (4 of 4).

In other months it is the opposite (momentum +0.7%, reversal −0.4%). This is the momentum crash the mentor's lesson
warns about; on 8 shares it looked contradicted, but on 484 it shows. The evidence is weak (4 months).

- **The lesson:** `org-after-deep-fall` reaches decisions in a HIGH or SEVERE market and momentum, reversal and trend
  members. A momentum decision on a bank in today's SEVERE market now gets three lessons: bank concentration, RBI
  days, and this one.
- **The exams:** `exam-candidate-not-proof` and `exam-momentum-after-deep-fall` check that the model uses the
  organisation's lessons. They run on the real model in the next model session.

## Awareness of the outside world: the market pulse (AWARE-01)

The owner asked for more awareness: which shares are rising and falling and why, and what the big players are doing.
The news already reports it ("Bandhan Bank shares jump 7% as Abu Dhabi's IHC explores stake", "₹6,500 crore FII
buying", "promoter sells stake…"). `market_pulse.py` reads every collected headline by fixed rules across the NIFTY
500 names ([market pulse](../market-pulse.md)).

**First 14 days:**
- 295 headlines read: 18 share moves with their reasons;
- FII buying of ₹6,500 crore into IT;
- 3 big-player items, all about companies outside the NIFTY 500 or unnamed;
- no unexplained move in our 8 shares.

**Review of the first run found five misreadings, all fixed and tested:**
- profit growth counted as a share move ("Netweb +780%");
- a broker's forecast counted as a move ("can soar 143%");
- Cyient DLM read as Cyient;
- the market cap's amount taken as the FIIs' ("₹4 lakh crore rally, ₹6,500 crore FII buying");
- a year-to-date figure attached to today's fall.

**Where it is used:**
- the debate sees it for the share under debate, as of the proposal;
- `npm run status` shows a Pulse line, and `npm run pulse` gives the detail;
- the cycle rebuilds it daily.

**Not built:** exchange filings (bulk and block deals, insider trades, pledges, FII and DII daily flows). They need an
approved source (founder review B15), and NSE and BSE restrict scraping.

**Scenario panel.** A day when every voice failed (the model was down) is no longer filed as 9 empty calls. The step
fails visibly and can rehearse again the same day. Its only run so far, on 27 Oct, was such a day, so it has no real
forecasts yet.
